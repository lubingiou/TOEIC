TOEIC TEST

In the Reading and Listening test, you will read a variety of texts and answer several different types of comprehension questions. You must mark your answers carefully.

PART 5: Incomplete Sentences

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence.

101. The new learning _______ introduced by the teacher makes English enjoyable for everyone.

正確答案：(B) technique。空格前為 learning (學習的)，應填名詞「方法/技巧」，technique 為名詞。

102. The supervisor wants to speak with the employee _______ the new marketing project.

正確答案：(C) leading。由關係子句簡化而來的分詞片語：The employee (who is) leading... 主動帶領，故用現在分詞 leading。

103. It's high time that our company _______ its strategy to attract more younger customers.

正確答案：(C) changed。文法：It's (high) time that + S + V-ed (該是...的時候了)，故動詞用過去式 changed。

104. There is no _______ that the talented basketball player will win the MVP award this year.

正確答案：(A) doubt。片語 There is no doubt that... (毫無疑問...)。

105. Some people find the instructions on the tax forms very _______ and difficult to understand.

正確答案：(C) confusing。形容詞「令人困惑的」，用來形容事物(instructions)。

106. In recognition of his lifetime _______ in sports, the coach was given a special award.

正確答案：(D) achievements。in recognition of his achievements (為了表揚他的成就)。

107. The new smartphone _______ younger consumers who want high-quality cameras.

正確答案：(A) targets。動詞「以...為目標」。這款新手機以追求高品質相機的年輕消費者為目標。

108. Because of the ongoing economic _______, many businesses have had to close down.

正確答案：(A) slump。經濟衰退/低迷 (economic slump)。

109. The documentary is about a _______ who successfully turned a failing company into a global industry leader.

正確答案：(B) politician。選項中只有 politician (政治人物) 符合語意。

110. Performing arts _______ help translate auditory information into sign language for deaf individuals.

正確答案：(A) interpreters。performing arts interpreters (表演藝術口譯員)。

111. _______ research of the performance is equally important for the interpreters to do a good job.

正確答案：(A) Prior。prior research (事前的研究)。

112. The dog _______ under the manager's desk is very quiet and friendly.

正確答案：(C) sleeping。分詞片語修飾名詞：The dog (that is) sleeping...，簡化為 sleeping。

113. The company is known _______ its exceptional customer service and high-quality products.

正確答案：(B) for。片語 be known for (以...聞名)。

114. Our manager's strong sense of _______ makes the stressful workplace much more enjoyable.

正確答案：(A) humor。sense of humor (幽默感)。

115. Getting a high salary is nice, but job satisfaction is also an important _______ to consider.

正確答案：(D) element。element (元素/要素)，工作滿意度也是一個重要的考量要素。

Questions 141-145 refer to the following article on taxes.

While we normally pay tax on our salaries, some countries have highly unusual targets. In Russia, Peter the Great introduced a "beard tax" in 1698 because he wanted his society to look more like Western Europe. Men with beards had to pay a tax, or else they had their beards forcibly shaved off.

In the US state of Maine, 99% of wild blueberries are grown there. Because they are proud of this charming industry, the local government introduced a blueberry tax. The funding from this tax goes toward research to help keep Maine's blueberry production the best in the world.

Additionally, the UK has a TV licence fee. The BBC receives about three-quarters of its funding from this fee, and anyone watching live broadcasts must pay it.

144. What does the BBC rely on for most of its funding?

Questions 146-150 refer to the following feature article.

When people attend concerts or musicals, they expect to be entertained. But for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals, enjoying a theatrical performance can be challenging. This is where performing arts interpreters come in.

Their job is not just translating words; they must translate the emotion and auditory elements, like music rhythm, into sign language. Prior research is very important. Interpreters need to be familiar with the story and often attend practices to ensure their speed matches the performance.

For instance, when an important action takes place, the interpreter directs the audience's attention to the performer or the scene. All this hard work is done so that everyone can equally engage in theatrical activities.
