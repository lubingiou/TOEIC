每日多益練習 - 0901單字會考

TOEIC TEST

In the Reading and Listening test, you will read a variety of texts and answer several different types of comprehension questions. You must mark your answers carefully.

1. Although Mr. Henderson has over fifteen years of managerial experience, he remains somewhat ------- about overseeing a multinational merger of this scale.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考形容詞選用與搭配。空格前有連繫動詞 "remains" 與程度副詞 "somewhat"，後接介系詞片語 "about..."，此處需要形容詞作為主詞補語。"apprehensive" 意為「擔心的、憂慮的」，常見搭配為 "be/remain apprehensive about..."。(A) 為現在分詞；(C) 為名詞；(D) 為副詞。

2. Passengers are politely requested to ------- from using cellular phones and other electronic devices during the entire duration of the presentation.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞句型與介系詞搭配。四個選項皆有「限制/禁止/克制」之意，但當主詞為「人」且後面搭配介系詞 "from + -ing" 時，應選不及物動詞 "refrain" (refrain from -ing，意為克制/避免做某事)。其餘選項皆為及物動詞：(B) restrict A to B；(C) prohibit A from -ing；(D) prevent A from -ing。

3. All sub-contractors on the construction site must strictly ------- to the newly established workplace safety protocols.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考同義動詞之介系詞搭配。四個選項皆表示「遵守」，但只有 "adhere" 必須搭配介系詞 "to" (adhere to + rules/protocols)。(A) comply 需搭配 with (comply with)；(C) observe 與 (D) follow 皆為及物動詞，直接接受詞（如 observe/follow the protocols）。

4. Employees who have completed at least two years of continuous service are ------- for the tuition reimbursement program.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考形容詞字義與介系詞搭配。空格後接介系詞 "for"，表達「有資格獲得/參加…」應選 "eligible" (be eligible for + N / be eligible to do)。(A) qualifying 若作形容詞表「合格的」，但動詞 qualify 常用 qualify for 且句型不同；(B) allowed 常接不定詞 (be allowed to do)；(D) capable 搭配 of (be capable of)。

5. Candidates applying for the executive directorship must demonstrate exceptional ------- ability and strategic vision.

正確答案：((D)) 本題考修飾名詞的形容詞選用。空格後面為名詞片語 "ability and strategic vision"，需要形容詞來修飾名詞 "ability"。"managerial" 為形容詞「管理的、經營上的」(managerial ability/skills/experience)。(C) management 為名詞，雖可作複合名詞，但 managerial 為修飾 ability 最道地且精準的選詞。

6. The sales representative scheduled a series of face-to-face meetings with ------- clients to present the updated software features.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考形近字與文意辨析。(A) prospective 為形容詞，意為「預期的、未來的、潛在的」，"prospective clients" 指「潛在客戶」；(B) perspective 為名詞「觀點、洞察力」；(C) prosperous 為形容詞「繁榮的、成功的」；(D) standard 為「標準的」。

7. The board approved a comprehensive severance package as ------- for employees affected by the upcoming factory restructuring.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考名詞字義與上下文搭配。句意為「董事會批准了一份完整的離職金方案，作為對受重組影響員工的『補償』」。「補償、報償」應選 "compensation" (compensation for)。(B) requirement 為必要條件；(C) allowance 為津貼/補貼；(D) wage 為按時或按週計算的工資。

8. Unauthorized personnel are strictly forbidden from gaining ------- to the server room where sensitive financial records are stored.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考名詞慣用搭配片語。"gain access to" 為固定高頻搭配，意為「獲得…的使用權/進入權」；其中 access 作名詞時為不可數名詞。(A) approach 表「處理方法/接近」；(C) entry 與 (D) entrance 雖有入口/進入之意，但不與 "gain ... to" 構成此特定商務慣用語。

9. Prior to the product's global release, quality assurance engineers will ------- test every component for potential hardware vulnerabilities.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考副詞語意與動詞搭配。句意為「在產品全球發佈前，品管工程師將『徹底地』測試每個組件」。「徹底地、完全地」應選 "thoroughly" (thoroughly test/inspect)。(A) drastically 大幅地/急劇地；(B) severely 嚴厲地/嚴重地；(D) highly 非常（常用於修飾形容詞如 highly qualified）。

10. Failure to ------- with the city's environmental protection standards may result in hefty fines and temporary closure of the plant.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考動詞介系詞搭配。(A) adhere 需搭配 to (adhere to)；(B) comply 需搭配 with (comply with)；(C) abide 需搭配 by (abide by)；(D) conform 可接 to 或 with，但與法規/標準搭配時以 "comply with the standards" 為商務英文最標準用法。

11. Demonstrating advanced ------- in at least two foreign languages is a key prerequisite for international diplomatic posts.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考詞性與名詞選用。空格位於形容詞 "advanced" 之後、介系詞 "in" 之前，需要名詞。"proficiency" 為不可數名詞，意為「精通、熟練度」(proficiency in)。(A) 為形容詞；(B) 為拼字錯誤；(D) 為副詞。

12. Full-time employees who have served the firm for over one year are ------- to six weeks of paid parental leave.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考動詞片語與介系詞搭配。"be entitled to + N/infinitive" 意為「有資格/權利獲得…」；(C) authorized 的常見句型為 be authorized to do sth (後接動詞原形，若接名詞需為 authorize sth for sb)；(A) titled 為「命名為」；(D) delegated 為「委派」。

13. The client signed a non-binding letter of intent, meaning they are under no legal ------- to purchase the property.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考名詞片語搭配。"under no obligation to do sth" 為多益高頻固定片語，意為「沒有義務/責任去做某事」。(A) condition 條件；(C) restriction 限制；(D) regulation 規定。

14. Due to recent supply chain disruptions, the estimated lead time for manufacturing electronic components has increased -------.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考修飾動詞 increase 的副詞搭配。「急劇地、大幅地」增加應選 "drastically" (drastically increase/alter)。(B) severely 多指受損或懲罰程度嚴重（如 severely damaged/punished）；(C) highly 修飾形容詞（如 highly competitive）；(D) densely 密集地。

15. The newly enacted federal guidelines ------- that all financial institutions implement two-factor authentication for online banking.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞句型與法規語意。"mandate that + S + (should) V" 意為「強制規定/命令…」，符合「新頒布的聯邦準則『強制要求』所有金融機構實施…」之句意。(B) allow 與 (D) permit 常用 allow/permit sb to do，較少接 that 子句；(C) request 表示請求，缺乏法令的強制力。

16. With the notable ------- of the regional sales director, all corporate department heads were present at the annual strategy summit.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考介系詞片語固定搭配。"with the exception of..." 為固定慣用語，意為「除了…之外 (排除某人/某物)」。(A) exclusion 排除；(C) omission 遺漏；(D) rejection 拒絕，皆無法與 with the... of 構成此特定片語。

17. Representatives from the Department of Labor arrived unannounced to conduct a routine safety ------- of the manufacturing facility.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考名詞選用與動詞搭配。"conduct an inspection" 表示「進行檢查/檢驗」。(B) inspector 是「檢查員（人）」，在此句意中係對設施進行「檢查（抽象事件）」，故選 (C) inspection。(A) 為動詞；(D) 為形容詞。

18. Members of the executive committee were deeply ------- by the detailed financial forecast presented by the junior analyst.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考分詞形容詞（主動與被動）。主詞為 "Members of the executive committee"（人），表達「人對…感到印象深刻」應選被動分詞形容詞 "impressed" (be impressed by/with)。(A) impressive 意為「令人印象深刻的」，通常修飾事物（如 an impressive achievement）。

19. Strict company policies clearly ------- employees from sharing confidential internal documents with third-party vendors.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考動詞句型與語意區分。(B) prohibit 句型為 "prohibit A from -ing" (禁止某人做某事)；(A) forbid 句型為 "forbid A to do sth"（不可接 from -ing）；(C) refrain 為不及物動詞 (refrain from -ing，主詞需為人)；(D) prevent 雖然也有 prevent A from -ing，但語意為「防止/阻礙」，而「公司政策明確禁止」首選 (B) prohibit。

20. The chief operating officer stressed the importance of following standard administrative ------- when submitting expense reports.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考同源名詞字義辨析。"standard administrative procedures"（標準行政程序/步驟）為商務領域固定用語。(B) processing 為「加工、處理（不可數名詞）」；(C) proceedings 為「訴訟程序、會議記錄」；(D) progression 為「進展、演變」。

21. After evaluating dozens of qualified applicants, the hiring committee identified Ms. Vance as the top ------- for the directorship.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考名詞字義辨析。(A) candidate 意為「應徵者、候選人」，"top candidate" 指「最佳人選」；(B) occupant 為「（房屋/職位的）佔有者、居住者」；(C) resident 為「居民」；(D) employer 為「雇主」。

22. Applicants are requested to submit three professional ------- who can vouch for their work ethic and technical competencies.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考名詞指「人」或指「物」之區分。句末有關係代詞 "who can vouch..."，表示前面名詞必須指「人」。指「推薦人（提供參考資訊的人）」時應用 "references"。(A) recommendation 指推薦信（物）；(C) credential 資格證明；(D) referral 轉介/推薦案。

23. Including seasonal workers hired for the holidays, the firm currently has over five hundred individuals listed on its -------.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考介系詞片語固定搭配。"on the payroll"（在薪水帳冊上、受僱於該公司）為多益極高頻之介系詞片語。wages, salary, compensation 皆不可與 "on its..." 搭配表達「在公司發薪名單上」之意。

24. Obtaining an advanced project management ------- can significantly enhance a candidate's career prospects in the tech sector.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考同源名詞抽象概念與具體名稱辨析。"professional/project management certification" 指取得「（資格）認證/檢定資格（抽象資格體系與過程）」；而 certificate 特指實體的「證書/執照」。此處強調管理領域的專業認證資格，故選 (C) certification。

25. To prevent unauthorized access, security protocols ------- entry to the data center to authorized IT personnel only.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考動詞句型與介系詞搭配。"restrict A to B" 意為「將 A 限制在 B 的範圍內」。句意為「將進入資料中心的權限僅限制於授權的 IT 人員」。(A) refrain 不及物動詞；(C) abolish 廢除；(D) ban 句型為 ban A from B。

26. The board of directors voted unanimously to ------- the mandatory weekend overtime policy after observing widespread employee fatigue.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞語意搭配。(A) abolish 意為「廢除（制度、法律、政策等）」；(B) enforce 實施/執行；(C) prohibit 禁止；(D) execute 執行。廢除不合時宜的加班政策應選 abolish。

27. Regulatory agencies possess the legal authority to ------- environmental regulations and issue fines to non-compliant corporations.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考動詞字義與主詞邏輯。句意為「監管機構擁有法律授權來『執行』環保法規」。「執行（法規、條例）」應選 "enforce" (enforce regulations/laws)。(A) obey 與 (B) comply 的主詞通常為受規範的公司或個人；(D) enact 是「制定（法律）」。

28. Dr. Arisawa has been closely ------- with the institute's renewable energy research initiative for nearly a decade.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞被動慣用法。"be associated with..." 意為「與…有關聯/參與…」。(B) 為動詞原形/名詞；(C) 為名詞；(D) 為現在分詞。

29. The sleek minimalist design of the new smartphone is expected to ------- strongly to tech-savvy young consumers.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考動詞介系詞搭配。(B) "appeal to sb" 意為「對某人有吸引力/引起興趣」。(A) attract, (C) entice, (D) persuade 皆為及物動詞，直接接受詞（如 attract consumers），不可加介系詞 to。

30. Before launching the rebranding campaign, the marketing director worked in close ------- with a renowned brand strategist.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考介系詞片語與名詞選用。"in consultation with sb" 為固定搭配，意為「與某人商討/諮詢」。(B) consultant 為「顧問（人）」，不能接在 in close... with 之中表達商討過程；(A) 為動詞；(D) 為形容詞。

31. The executive search firm was retained to ------- top-tier talent for the newly created chief technology officer position.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞字義與搭配。"recruit talent/employees" 表示「招募人才/員工」。(B) apply 用法為 apply for 職位；(C) qualify 使合格；(D) enroll 註冊/報名（課程）。

32. Fluency in Mandarin is a mandatory ------- for all sales representatives assigned to the East Asian regional offices.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考名詞選用。空格前有形容詞 "mandatory"（強制的、必須的），後需要名詞。"requirement for..." 意為「…的必要條件/要求」。(A) 為動詞；(B) 為過去分詞；(D) 多作形容詞。

33. The event coordinator made all necessary ------- for the international conference, including venue booking and catering.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考動詞與名詞慣用搭配。"make arrangements for..." 意為「為…做出安排/準備」。(B) preparing 詞性不合；(C) agreement 是協議；(D) condition 是條件。

34. Shareholder representatives expressed grave ------- about the company's declining operating margins during the quarterly meeting.

正確答案：((B)) 本題考名詞選用與介系詞搭配。動詞 "expressed" 後面需要名詞作為受詞，且搭配介系詞 "about/over"。「表達擔憂」應選 "express concern about"。(A) anxious, (C) problematic, (D) nervous 皆為形容詞。

35. Employees attending the client gala are expected to wear formal business ------- appropriate for an evening event.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考名詞可數性與商務用語特質。"business attire" 為不可數名詞，專指正式商務服裝，前面無冠詞 a/an。(B) clothes, (C) garments, (D) outfits 皆為可數名詞複數，在此句中無冠詞修飾時，最符合商務正式語境的選詞為 (A) attire。

36. The newly enacted tax regulations will come into ------- on the first day of the next fiscal quarter.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考介系詞片語固定搭配。"come into effect" 或 "take effect" 表示「（法律、規定）生效」。(A) affect 是動詞；(B) effective 是形容詞；(D) effectively 是副詞。

37. Any staff member found guilty of violating confidentiality agreements will be ------- disciplined by management.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考修飾動詞 discipline/punish 的副詞選用。「嚴厲地懲戒/處分」應選 "severely" (severely disciplined/punished)。(B) drastically 多修飾數據/數量的急劇變化 (drastically reduced/increased)；(C) highly 修飾讚美或資格 (highly praised/qualified)；(D) thoroughly 表示徹底地。

38. Due to the system's frequent glitches, the technical support desk received a ------- stream of complaints from frustrated users.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考形容詞修飾名詞。空格後修飾名詞片語 "stream of complaints"，需要形容詞。"a constant stream of..." 意為「源源不絕的、持續不斷的…」。(B) 為副詞；(C) 與 (D) 為名詞。

39. The government introduced new ------- aimed at curbing aggressive corporate tax avoidance practices.

正確答案：((C)) 本題考名詞選用。句意為「政府提出了新的『立法/法律法規』」。"legislation" 為不可數名詞，意為「立法、法規」。(A) 為動詞；(B) 為「立法議員（人）」；(D) 為形容詞。

40. Regular exercise and adequate sleep should become a daily ------- for employees seeking to maintain peak operational performance.

正確答案：((A)) 本題考近義名詞辨析。(A) habit 指「個人的生活習慣/習性」(daily habit)；(B) convention 指「社會/公司慣例、常規」；(C) tradition 指「文化傳統」；(D) custom 指「社會風俗/習俗」。此處指個人保持健康的習慣，應選 (A) habit。
